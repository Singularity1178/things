Twilight bled across the southern edge of the Great Forest of Tob, turning the dirt road near Carne Village into a ribbon of deep shadow and bruised violet light. The air smelled of damp pine needles, churned earth, and distant hearth smoke.

Along that quiet track walked a lone traveler. A travel-worn cloak of muted slate gray hung loosely over his frame, its high collar pulled up to meet a smooth, featureless porcelain mask with narrow slits for his dark, unreadable eyes. Beneath that unassuming exterior, however, lay an existence that no longer belonged to the standard rules of YGGDRASIL. Upon being dragged into this world, he had expended the World Item **Ouroboros** to rewrite the core parameters of his avatar—shedding the ceiling of an ordinary Level 100 player to become a **World Enemy**, a calamity-class existence normally meant to be fought by thirty max-level players at once.

Yet he had no interest in conquest or slaughter. Every shred of his monstrous presence was locked tight behind layered concealment—`[False Data: Life]`, `[False Data: Mana]`, and passive suppression skills that flattened his aura into that of an unremarkable, fragile human wanderer. He was simply walking, observing the geography of an unfamiliar world and steering clear of trouble.

Perched high in the thick boughs of an oak overlooking the road, Lupusregina Beta tilted her head, her braided reddish-brown hair shifting against her maid uniform. Having been strictly ordered to keep watch over the perimeter of Carne Village—and keenly aware of the consequences of failing to report suspicious activity—her sharp werewolf senses had locked onto the stranger the moment he entered the clearing.

She sniffed the breeze lightly. Ordinary human scent. No mana signature. No martial aura. Just a weirdo in a mask strolling far too close to a village she was supposed to keep secret.

Lupusregina dropped from the branch without a sound, landing squarely in the middle of the dirt path ten paces ahead of him. Her massive, ornate crozier rested casually across one shoulder as she flashed a bright, teasing smile.

"Yahho~! Good evening, Mr. Traveler! Kinda late to be taking a stroll right next to a scary forest, isn't it~?"

The traveler came to a calm halt. Behind the porcelain mask, his eyes swept over her once. Even through heavy concealment, the innate perception of a World Enemy read the truth of her existence immediately: a Level 59 heteromorph, equipped with high-grade divine caster gear, radiating the unmistakable blueprint of YGGDRASIL. A subordinate of another player, or an NPC left behind by one.

Fighting her offered zero benefit and risked alerting whoever held her leash.

"I have no business here," the traveler said, his voice level, polite, and entirely devoid of hostility. "I am only passing through. I will turn back and leave you in peace."

He pivoted smoothly, preparing to walk back the way he had come.

Lupusregina's cheerful smile stayed plastered on her face, though her golden eyes narrowed with sharp calculation. He hadn't flinched when she dropped out of nowhere. He hadn't trembled at the sight of a monstrously heavy weapon in a maid's hands. Worse, if an unauthorized stranger had spotted her guarding this specific stretch of road, she couldn't afford to let him wander back toward Re-Estize or the Slane Theocracy to gossip. Besides, the casual way he dismissed her grated on her sadistic streak.

Her bubbly tone dropped an octave, warmth curdling into cold amusement. "Ehh~? Leaving already? Sorry, Mr. Mask, once someone wanders onto my turf, I don't really do refunds."

She kicked off the dirt with explosive werewolf agility, blurring across the ten paces in a fraction of a second as she layered `[Aspect of the Wolf]` and `[Physical Boost]`. She didn't bother wasting high-tier divine spells on a Level 1 human; her plan was simple—shatter his ribs and legs with the flat of her crozier, drag him into the undergrowth, and slowly peel away his secrets before finishing him off.

Her heavy crozier whistled through the dusk in a lethal, crushing horizontal arc, carrying enough kinetic force to pulp an adamantite-ranked warrior on impact.

Behind his mask, the traveler let out a quiet, resigned breath. *So she won't let me walk away.*

He didn't draw a weapon. He simply raised one gloved forearm into the path of the incoming strike and lowered a microscopic fraction of `[False Data: Life]`, allowing his natural physical defense to engage.

**CLANG.**

The impact rang through the forest like a cathedral bell struck by a falling star. Lupusregina's crozier didn't just stop—it rebounded violently, the shockwave numbing her hands to the wrists and tearing the earth beneath her boots into deep furrows. The traveler hadn't shifted a single millimeter. Not even the hem of his gray cloak stirred.

Lupusregina's sadistic grin vanished instantly.

Her werewolf ears flattened against her skull as every instinct in her body screamed in unison. Through that tiny, deliberate crack in his concealment, an abyssal, suffocating pressure leaked out—not the sharp mana of a spellcaster or the keen edge of a warrior, but the vast, crushing weight of an existence that stood far above even the Floor Guardians. A **World Enemy**.

Pure, primal terror overrode every ounce of her playful cruelty in a heartbeat. She propelled herself backward in a frantic leap, triggering `[Lion's Heart]` to burn away the paralyzing fear gripping her chest while her free hand snapped up toward her ear to cast `[Message]`—*Lord Ainz, an unknown—*

The traveler saw her hand rise to her ear and recognized the activation posture of `[Message]` immediately. If that transmission went through, whoever commanded her would know a World Enemy was walking their border.

He did not give her the half-second needed to speak a single word.

His right hand slipped beneath his cloak, his eyes flaring coldly behind the porcelain mask as his burst-damage build engaged all at once—stacking `[Crimson Overdrive]`, `[Limit Break: Strike]`, and `[Piercing Absolute]` into his signature finisher: **`[Severance of the Ninth Star]`**.

Space itself tore open in a razor-thin, blinding line of compressed violet-white light. Built to erase Level 100 players in a single strike and carve seventy percent out of maxed-out vanguard tanks, the burst struck a Level 59 cleric with the absolute finality of a guillotine dropping on thin glass.

Her `[Message]` never completed. Her enchanted maid uniform, divine wards, and werewolf regeneration offered zero resistance—vaporizing instantaneously as the burst cleaved through the air, reducing her entire physical form into silent, drifting motes of golden-red light before her heels even touched the ground from her backward leap.

Silence settled back over the darkening trees. The scorched seam across the dirt road cooled in the evening breeze.

The traveler adjusted his cloak, pulled `[False Data: Life]` and `[False Data: Mana]` back to full suppression, and continued his walk down the quiet path as if nothing had stood in his way at all.
