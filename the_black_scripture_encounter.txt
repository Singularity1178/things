The wind moving through the outer canopy of the Great Forest of Tob carried a scent that did not belong there.

Lupusregina Beta paused on a thick moss-covered branch, her red eyes narrowing beneath her wide maid's cap. Draped in `[Invisibility]` and relying on her natural werewolf senses, she inhaled slowly. Beneath the crisp tang of pine and damp earth, there was the faintest trace of iron, ozone, and something cold—an unnatural pressure clinging to an intruder moving along the forest floor forty meters below.

*An adventurer? No, adventurers smell like cheap sweat and bad leather,* she thought, a slow, sadistic grin curling across her lips. *Maybe a spy from the Slane Theocracy or the Empire sniffing around Carne Village?*

Ainz-sama had made it painfully clear what happened if she let threats slip past her perimeter again. Still, if this was just some overconfident scout who wandered into the wrong woods, breaking them a little before reporting back sounded like a wonderful way to pass the afternoon.

Below, Antilene Heran Fouche moved without a sound. Dressed in the sleek, light-plated **Armor of the Wind God** and carrying the massive black war-scythe **Charon's Guidance** across her back, the Extra Seat of the Black Scripture kept her heterochromatic eyes—one pitch black, one stark white—scanning the treeline. Her orders were strict: conduct solo covert reconnaissance along the borders of the Sorcerer Kingdom, verify the deployment of the Sorcerer King's forces, and withdraw without leaving a trace. Engaging the undead ruler's legions alone was suicide, even for her.

Then, a cheerful voice chimed from the branches directly ahead.

"Yoohoo~ You're awfully far from home, little half-elf. Did you take a wrong turn, or are you trying to sneak into Ainz-sama's backyard?"

Lupusregina dropped `[Invisibility]` as she landed lightly on the forest path ten paces away, resting the head of her ornate golden crozier over one shoulder with a bubbly, teasing smile.

Antilene froze. In a fraction of a second, her gaze took in every detail: the black-and-white battle maid uniform matching the Black Scripture's intelligence reports on the Sorcerer King's personal retainers, the unnatural poise of a non-human, and the fact that this maid had tracked her despite her stealth.

If this maid escaped or sent word to the Sorcerer King, the Slane Theocracy's covert operation—and possibly the nation itself—would be exposed to the monster who slaughtered hundreds of thousands at Katze Plains.

There was no banter. No boast. No wasted breath.

Antilene's fingers tightened around the haft of **Charon's Guidance** as she triggered `[Flow Acceleration]` and `[Greater Ability Boost]`.

The air cracked like a whip.

Lupusregina's playful smile vanished the exact instant Antilene's aura flared. The suffocating, bottomless pressure radiating from the half-elf's body and ancient relics hit Lupusregina like a physical blow. It wasn't the aura of a strong human hero—it was the terrifying density of a monster standing on the same tier as the Floor Guardians of Nazarick.

*Level eighty—no, higher! I have to warn Ainz-sama!*

Lupusregina kicked off the dirt in a desperate backward leap, her werewolf musculature surging as she simultaneously threw up a defensive barrier with `[Protection Energy]` and raised two fingers to her temple to cast `[Message]`.

She never finished the spell.

Antilene crossed the ten-pace gap before Lupusregina's boots even touched the ground. Moving far beyond what a Level 59 werewolf's reflexes could track, the God-kin blurred inside Lupusregina's guard and snapped out a low-tier cleric-inquisitor spell she had honed specifically for silencing casters: `[Silence]`.

The invisible field slammed over Lupusregina, swallowing the vocal component of `[Message]` into dead air.

In the same fluid motion, Antilene swung **Charon's Guidance** horizontally. The dark blade cleaved straight through `[Protection Energy]` as if it were wet paper, biting deep across Lupusregina's torso. Even with the high-tier defensive data crystals woven into her maid uniform, a torrent of blood erupted from the gash as the sheer physical disparity—twenty-nine levels separating a hybrid Level 88 God-kin from a Level 59 battle maid—launched Lupusregina backward into the trunk of an ancient oak.

Splinters exploded outward. Lupusregina hit the dirt, coughing blood, her carefree mask completely gone. Cold, feral survival instinct took over. She couldn't speak inside the `[Silence]` zone, and she couldn't outrun an opponent this fast. Her only choice was to force space open and buy a single second to escape the field.

Snarling, her fangs elongating and claws ripping through her gloves, Lupusregina activated `[Regeneration]` alongside silent-casted `[Bless]` and `[Divine Power]`, surging forward with her golden crozier raised in both hands. As she swung the heavy divine weapon in a crushing downward arc, she unleashed `[Holy Smite]` at point-blank range, flooding the clearing in searing white light.

Antilene didn't even flinch.

The **Armor of the Wind God** dispersed the kinetic shockwave, and her innate magic defense absorbed the mid-tier divine radiance as little more than an irritating glare. She parried the golden crozier effortlessly with the shaft of her scythe, the impact jarring Lupusregina's wrists to the bone.

*So this is the limit of a servant,* Antilene thought coldly, gauging the maid's strength in a single exchange. *Stronger than the Black Scripture's frontline, yet far weaker than the monsters I came here to scout. She isn't worth revealing my trump card.*

Before Lupusregina could recover from the parry, Antilene stepped into her blind spot with `[Shadow Step]`, stacking `[Ability Boost]` onto her assassin and executioner arts.

Lupusregina's wide red eyes barely registered the silver-and-black blur beside her throat. She tried to twist away, swinging her clawed hand blindly, only for Antilene to duck beneath it and execute `[Executioner's Strike]`.

The crescent blade of **Charon's Guidance** flashed once.

Lupusregina's head separated cleanly from her shoulders, her body collapsing forward onto the blood-soaked moss before her `[Regeneration]` could even begin to knit the severed tissue. The golden crozier slipped from her lifeless fingers and struck the earth with a dull, heavy thud.

Antilene didn't linger to admire the kill. Knowing full well that high-level beings from the Sorcerer Kingdom might carry resurrection items or fake-death contingencies, she pointed a hand at the fallen corpse and cast `[Death]` to ensure the soul was severed completely. Only when the body remained utterly still did she lower her scythe, wipe the blood from the blade with a flick of her wrist, and vanish back into the deep shadows of the forest before Nazarick ever realized one of its maids had gone silent.
