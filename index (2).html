BA Students tModLoader mod

Replaces every town NPC with a Blue Archive student. Your browser downloads each .glb from lihaohong6/BlueArchiveModels, bakes the Cafe_Idle / Cafe_Walk / Formation_Idle / attack clips into sprite sheets, and packs them with the C# code into a .zip you unzip into ModSources. Models missing a clip fall back to another one (see the log).

NPC → student list (untick to skip one)
