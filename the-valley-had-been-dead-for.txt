The valley had been dead for centuries, choked by gray ash and stripped of wind, yet the air above it hummed with a suffocating pressure that made even Zesshi Zetsumei narrow her heterochromatic eyes. Perched on a jagged ridge overlooking the basin, Antilene Heran Fouche rested the haft of `[Charon's Guidance]` against her shoulder, her gaze fixed on the colossal, shifting mound filling the gorge below. It looked like a small mountain range at first glance, until one noticed the way its surface writhed—millions of desiccated corpses, human and demi-human alike, packed together into a pulsing, necrotic sphere.

The Slane Theocracy’s Thousand Leagues Astrologer had detected the anomaly weeks ago: a concentration of negative energy so dense it warped divination spells. Fearing the awakening of an ancient catastrophe—or worse, one of the True Dragon Lords the Six Great Gods had warned against—the Cardinals had deployed their ultimate trump card. Outfitted in the pristine, divine-class armor of the Six Gods, Zesshi had come alone. Her orders were to confirm the threat and eradicate it if possible. Her personal motive was simpler. If the creature beneath those corpses was weak, she would butcher it and return to her quiet apathy. If it was strong enough to kill her, then after centuries of waiting, she would finally meet someone worthy of taking her life.

Zesshi did not announce herself or waste breath on challenges. She stepped off the ridge, activating `[Fly]` to glide silently toward the basin floor, and flicked her scythe downward. A crescent of compressed force—`[Void Cutter]`—streaked through the dead air and tore a thirty-meter trench straight through the outer crust of the corpse mound. Thousands of zombies disintegrated into dust and severed limbs.

For three heartbeats, nothing moved. Then, the entire valley floor erupted.

Deep within the shifting mass of undead, Cure Elim Los Malvar woke from his centuries-long brooding. At first, the Elder Coffin Dragon Lord felt only sluggish irritation at the disturbance. Then his hollow eye sockets locked onto the petite half-elf hovering above his domain—and more importantly, onto the radiant, unmistakable aura of the divine-class gear she wore and the deathly authority radiating from `[Charon's Guidance]`.

Cold, suffocating terror spiked through Cure Elim’s undead mind, instantly followed by venomous hatred. *Filth of the Dragon Emperor.* He knew that aura. It was the stench of Players—the same cursed monsters who had slaughtered the True Dragon Lords and driven him into hiding in the dark corners of the world. He had spent centuries harvesting the souls of entire nations precisely because he feared this day. He would not speak to her. He would not test her. He had to annihilate her before she could summon others.

With a deafening roar that shook loose boulders from the cliffs, Cure Elim surged upward. Roughly one hundred and fifty meters of skeletal, withered dragon bulk remained hidden inside a roaring hurricane of over a million zombies, turning him into a living fortress of rotting flesh. Without a second of hesitation, he swung a titanic, zombie-encrusted forelimb across the gorge while releasing `[Negative Burst]` to flood the air with lethal negative energy.

Zesshi’s pupils contracted as she gauged the sheer scale of the monster. **Level ninety or higher**, her instincts screamed, far surpassing the Platinum Dragon Lord’s armor in raw mass and necrotic density. She triggered `[Flow Acceleration]` and `[Ability Boost]`, darting backward as the colossal limb shattered the cliffside she had just vacated. The wave of `[Negative Burst]` washed over her, only to disperse harmlessly against the high-tier resistances of the Six Gods' armor.

Instead of retreating, Zesshi dashed straight into the storm of undead. With `[Greater Ability Boost]` augmenting her already superhuman strength, she spun `[Charon's Guidance]` in blistering arcs. Every sweep cleaved through hundreds of zombies at once, their low-level claws snapping uselessly against her divine plate. She carved a tunnel deep into the writhing sphere, aiming for the dragon’s true body within—only for the zombies to surge back together like water, grasping at her limbs, obscuring her vision, and cushioning the dragon’s scales beneath layers of packed corpses.

A tail the size of a fortress tower slammed into her flank through the blind spot of the swarm. Even with `[Reinforce Armor]` active, the sheer physical force sent Zesshi rocketing across the basin like a meteor, smashing through three stone pillars before she arrested her momentum mid-air. Blood trickled from the corner of her lip. Her arms throbbed beneath her gauntlets.

Zesshi’s expression remained entirely blank as she wiped the blood away, running a cold, pragmatic calculation. *It’s too massive, and there are too many.* Against a Level 95 True Dragon Lord shrouded in over a million meat shields, a prolonged melee was suicide. Her stamina and mana would bleed dry long before she could hack through enough corpses to inflict fatal wounds on the dragon's core. Just as she had concluded against the dark elf intruder in the Elf King's palace, she could not win a war of attrition. She had to end it with a single, unavoidable strike.

She raised her scythe, invoking her Valkyrie class trump card: **`[Einherjar]`**.

Beside her, space distorted and solidified into an exact, silent duplicate of Zesshi—clad in identical divine armor and holding a replica of `[Charon's Guidance]`. While the construct possessed none of her spells or skills, its physical parameters matched hers completely. Without a word, the `[Einherjar]` shot forward like a bullet, plunging back into the zombie vortex to draw the dragon's focus.

Simultaneously, the real Zesshi activated her innate Talent, drawing upon the residual power of Surshana bound within `[Charon's Guidance]`. Necrotic energy coiled around her as she triggered **`[The Goal of All Life is Death]`**. Behind her back, the translucent, ghostly silhouette of a massive clock face materialized, its hand beginning a slow, inexorable twelve-second sweep. All she needed to do was survive until the hand struck twelve and release `[Cry of the Banshee]`; the amplified instant-death magic would bypass every undead immunity and kill the dragon outright.

Inside the storm of corpses, Cure Elim felt the construct tearing into his flank, slashing deep gouges across his lower ribs before he crushed it beneath thousands of piling zombies and a crushing downward stomp. The dragon paused for a fraction of a second as the shattered duplicate dissolved into mist rather than bleeding. *A decoy.*

His burning sockets snapped upward, piercing the haze of ash to spot the real half-elf hovering a hundred meters away—and the eerie, ticking clock face glowing behind her shoulders.

Cure Elim did not know what the clock did. He did not care to find out. Driven by centuries of paranoia and a cowardly refusal to ever risk his own existence against a Player’s unknown trump card, the Elder Coffin Dragon Lord made his choice in an instant. He had hoarded millions of souls for this exact moment.

Opening his cavernous, skeletal jaws, Cure Elim drew upon hundreds of thousands of trapped souls burning within his chest. Violet-black light coalesced in his throat, warping the air and draining the color from the surrounding stone.

Zesshi’s heterochromatic eyes widened slightly. Six seconds remained on the clock behind her. Too long. Even without knowing the mechanics of Wild Magic, every survival instinct honed over centuries screamed that the light gathering in the dragon’s maw was an existential threat—something her divine-class armor could not block and her agility could not outrun in time. She began chanting `[Greater Teleportation]` to escape the line of fire, knowing it would abort her trump card.

She never finished the first syllable.

Cure Elim unleashed **`[Soulbreaker Breath]`**.

A blinding, torrential beam of black-and-violet Wild Magic tore across the valley at impossible speed, widening as it traveled to swallow the entire airspace where Zesshi stood. There was no explosion, no clash of energies, and no resistance. Possessing no World Item to shield her, Zesshi’s divine armor, her half-elf resilience, and her soul were erased simultaneously the instant the beam touched her. She vanished without even leaving ash behind, the ghostly clock behind her flickering out into nothingness.

The beam carved a miles-long, glassed trench into the distant mountains before Cure Elim finally snapped his jaws shut, his massive undead frame shuddering as the immense expenditure of souls echoed through his core. For long minutes, the Elder Coffin Dragon Lord remained frozen beneath his shifting cloak of a million corpses, his hollow eyes scanning the empty, silent sky in frantic paranoia, waiting for allies that would never come.
