The wind across the ruined clearing carried the faint scent of ozone and old blood, stirring the ends of Antilene Heran Fouche’s two-toned hair. Standing twenty paces away, clad in ornate crimson full-plate armor that gleamed like freshly spilled gore, was a petite girl with silver hair and eyes the color of rubies.

Zesshi Zetsumei did not speak. Her heterochromatic eyes—one black, one white—swept over her opponent with practiced, clinical detachment. She registered the towering, grotesque lance gripped in the vampire’s pale hands, the overwhelming pressure radiating from the crimson armor, and the unmistakable, suffocating aura of an undead entity far beyond anything recorded in the Slane Theocracy’s archives.

Across from her, Shalltear Bloodfallen tilted her head, a cruel smile curving her lips, though her ruby eyes remained razor-sharp. Once, she might have treated a native of this world as an insect to be toyed with. That mistake had nearly cost her everything—and worse, had forced her beloved Supreme Being to strike her down. Never again. Looking at the dark, ominous armor of Surshana draped over the half-elf’s frame and the massive, wicked scythe resting in her grip, Shalltear immediately recognized the unmistakable aura of YGGDRASIL relics.

*Equipment left behind by Players,* Shalltear thought, her posture tightening imperceptibly. *Just like the filth who carried that world-class item. I will not underestimate her.*

Zesshi moved first. She did not waste breath on declarations or taunts. Her boots tore into the earth as she stacked Martial Arts in seamless succession—`[Flow Acceleration]`, `[Ability Boost]`, and `[Greater Ability Boost]`—turning herself into a blur of black and white. She closed the distance in a heartbeat, swinging *Charon’s Guidance* in a horizontal arc aimed directly at Shalltear’s neck, layering `[True Severance]` onto the blade while simultaneously triggering the scythe’s innate `[Death]` effect.

Steel met crimson metal with a deafening shockwave that flattened the surrounding grass.

Shalltear hadn’t dodged; she had raised the shaft of the *Spuit Lance* to catch the scythe’s crescent blade. Sparks showered over them as the ground fractured beneath Shalltear’s armored heels. The `[Death]` effect washed over the True Vampire and vanished without even making her flinch, negated entirely by her racial immunities.

Zesshi’s pupils contracted slightly. *Instant death negated. Physical strength… superior to mine, even with `[Greater Ability Boost]`.*

Before Zesshi could disengage, Shalltear twisted her wrists, deflecting the scythe upward, and thrust the *Spuit Lance* forward in a blinding counterattack. Zesshi barely managed to activate `[Fortress]` and angle her forearm guard to parry, yet the sheer kinetic force still punched through her defense, carving a deep, jagged furrow across her ribs and hurling her backward through the air.

Zesshi flipped mid-air, skidding across the dirt on her boots to arrest her momentum. Blood soaked into the dark fabric beneath her armor. Worse than the pain was what she saw across the clearing: the faint scratch her `[True Severance]` had left on Shalltear’s cheek was already gone, and the tip of that grotesque syringe-like lance pulsed with a dark, satisfied glow.

*She heals when she inflicts damage,* Zesshi calculated coldly, her mind racing through every variable. *Her raw physical parameters exceed mine by at least ten levels. Her equipment is divine-tier or close to it. If I fight a war of attrition, I will bleed out while she remains at full strength.*

A flicker of dark satisfaction crossed Shalltear’s face as she tasted the residual lifeforce channeled through her weapon, yet she ruthlessly suppressed the rising urge of `[Blood Frenzy]`. She kept her mind clear, watching the half-elf’s unnervingly calm expression. *She took a direct blow from the Spuit Lance and didn’t even cry out. And that strange energy she used to boost her speed wasn’t mana. What is she?*

"You wear the gear of Players, yet you move with the strange tricks of this world," Shalltear said, her aristocratic voice crisp and wary as she raised her lance. "No matter. I shall tear you apart before you can use whatever hidden card you are clutching!"

Shalltear cast `[Time Accelerator]` and vanished.

Zesshi did not try to match her speed. She had already reached the only logical conclusion available to her. Against an opponent superior in every conventional metric, prolonged melee was suicide. She needed her absolute winning condition, and she needed twelve seconds to execute it.

Just as Shalltear materialized behind her, lance descending in a lethal downward cleave, Zesshi triggered her class trump card: `[Einherjar]`.

Space rippled beside Zesshi, and a second, identical Zesshi stepped into existence—wearing the exact same dark armor, wielding an identical replica of *Charon’s Guidance*, and possessing the exact same physical capabilities.

The duplicate intercepted Shalltear’s strike with a two-handed parry, taking the full brunt of the Level 100 Floor Guardian’s blow. The impact cratered the earth, shattering stones into dust, while the real Zesshi used the split-second collision to leap thirty paces back, skidding to a halt.

Shalltear’s eyes widened in genuine shock. *`[Einherjar]`?! That is a Valkyrie’s trump card! How does a creature of this world possess the same skill as me?!*

If the enemy had a duplicate, leaving it unchecked while chasing the original would invite a fatal flank. Shalltear did not hesitate. She immediately activated her own `[Einherjar]`, summoning a crimson-armored construct of herself that surged forward to lock blades with Zesshi’s duplicate. A Level 88 construct against a Level 100 construct was a mismatch from the start; Shalltear’s Einherjar relentlessly battered Zesshi’s copy backward, forcing it onto the desperate defensive.

Satisfied that her construct would crush the decoy, Shalltear turned her gaze back to the real Zesshi—and froze.

Zesshi had planted the butt of *Charon’s Guidance* into the earth. Drawing upon her innate Talent to invoke the trump card of the scythe’s original owner, Surshana, she spoke softly into the roaring wind.

"`[The Goal of All Life is Death]`."

Behind the half-elf, the air fractured, and an enormous, spectral clock face materialized in the sky, its ornate hands beginning a slow, inexorable tick.

A cold chill—something a True Vampire had no biological right to feel—shot straight down Shalltear’s spine. Her breath hitched. Every detail of that phantom clock, the suffocating aura of absolute, unavoidable annihilation radiating from it, was burned permanently into her memory.

*Lord Ainz’s trump card!*

Panic threatened to flare in her chest, immediately followed by the hard-won clarity of her defeat in the forest outside E-Rantel. She knew what that clock meant. She knew its exact mechanics better than anyone alive in this world. In twelve seconds, whatever instant-death magic Zesshi paired with it would bypass every undead immunity she possessed, killing her outright—forcing her to burn the precious resurrection ring Lord Peroroncino had gifted her.

*Twelve seconds. She needs twelve seconds for the hand to complete its circuit!*

Zesshi watched the crimson vampire, expecting confusion or blind brute force, ready to cast `[Death]` the moment the ritual primed. Instead, she saw the vampire’s ruby eyes narrow with sudden, terrifying comprehension.

Shalltear did not waste a tenth of a second. She bypassed the clashing duplicates entirely with `[Greater Teleportation]`, appearing directly in front of Zesshi just four seconds into the countdown.

Zesshi reacted with inhuman reflexes, swinging *Charon’s Guidance* in a desperate horizontal sweep empowered by `[Flow Acceleration]` to force the vampire back. Shalltear didn’t bother dodging. She triggered `[Negative Impact Shield]`, shattering the momentum of Zesshi’s scythe in a burst of kinetic backlash that wrenched the weapon wide and left Zesshi’s torso completely exposed.

"I will not fall to that trick twice!" Shalltear snarled.

At point-blank range, Shalltear unleashed a 9th-tier spell: `[Vermilion Nova]`.

A pillar of blinding, concentrated crimson fire erupted directly over Zesshi, exploiting the dark, death-aspected affinity of Surshana’s vestments. Zesshi’s scream was drowned out by the roar of the flames as her armor scorched and cracked under the overwhelming magical damage. Before the smoke could even clear, Shalltear followed up with `[Purifying Lance]`, driving a spear of searing holy light straight through Zesshi’s left shoulder, pinning her momentum.

Six seconds on the clock.

Half-blinded, burned, and bleeding profusely, Zesshi gritted her teeth through the agony and forced her vocal cords to work, casting the spell that would complete the combo: "`[Death]`—"

She never finished the syllable.

Shalltear activated `[Time Accelerator]` once more, closing the last two meters in a flash of crimson steel. The *Spuit Lance* punched straight through Zesshi’s chest, shattering her breastplate and impaling her heart before emerging cleanly out her back.

Zesshi coughed a mouthful of blood onto the silver shaft of the lance. Her heterochromatic eyes flickered upward toward the sky, where the phantom clock had barely passed the eight-second mark. As the life drained rapidly from her body into Shalltear’s weapon, the spectral clock behind her fractured like glass, dissolving into harmless motes of light.

Across the clearing, Zesshi’s `[Einherjar]` vanished into mist a heartbeat later, followed by Shalltear’s own construct dismissing itself.

Shalltear wrenched the *Spuit Lance* free, letting Zesshi’s lifeless body crumple silently into the scorched earth. The True Vampire stood motionless for several seconds, her chest rising and falling slowly even though she had no need to breathe, watching the corpse carefully to ensure no resurrection magic triggered.

When nothing stirred, Shalltear lowered her lance, the bloodlust fading into a cold, sober certainty. This world was far more dangerous than Nazarick had initially believed—and she was silently grateful that she had learned her lesson before it was too late.
