Moonlight filtered sparingly through the dense canopy of the Great Forest of Tob, casting silver-gray streaks across the damp undergrowth a few miles north of Carne Village.

Hovering twenty feet above the forest floor under the effect of **[Fly]**, Evileye kept her magical aura tightly suppressed. Beneath her white porcelain mask, her crimson eyes scanned the treeline with the practiced caution of someone who had survived two hundred years in a world full of monsters. Ever since the Sorcerer Kingdom had claimed E-Rantel, Blue Rose had been operating on a razor's edge—gathering intelligence while avoiding direct confrontation with the terrifying entities tied to Ainz Ooal Gown and the demon Jaldabaoth. Tonight, Evileye had separated from Lakyus and the others to scout the perimeter near a supposedly unremarkable border village that had inexplicably survived the surrounding chaos.

A faint rustle of leaves broke the nocturnal silence.

Evileye froze mid-air, her hand instinctively tightening beneath her crimson cloak. There was no sound of heavy armor, no crackling mana signature—yet the hairs on the back of her neck stood on end.

"Well, well, well," a bright, melodic voice echoed from the shadows below. "What's a creepy little vampire doing sniffing around out here in the middle of the night?"

Evileye dropped lower behind the cover of a thick oak bough, peering down as a tall woman stepped into a patch of moonlight. She had tanned skin, braided auburn hair, and a playful, almost carefree posture. Across her shoulder rested an enormous, ornate crozier capped with a gleaming silver head and crimson ornamentation.

Then Evileye saw what the woman was wearing: an intricate, high-collared maid uniform accented with black and gold armor plates.

Evileye’s breath hitched—a purely habitual reflex, as her undead lungs needed no air. *That uniform.* It was identical to the attire worn by the insectoid monster maid who had nearly slaughtered Gagaran and Tia during the Demon Disturbance in the royal capital. A subordinate of Jaldabaoth. One of the walking calamities capable of matching adamantite-rank adventurers single-handedly.

Down on the forest floor, Lupusregina Beta tilted her head, her amber eyes narrowing as her werewolf senses dissected the intruder. The scent of dry dust, stagnant blood, and centuries-old magic clung to the small figure floating in the trees. More importantly, the red cloak and featureless white mask matched the exact description Entoma had screeched about for weeks.

Lupusregina’s cheerful smile widened, though the warmth vanished from her eyes entirely. *The adventurer who took Entoma’s voice.*

Normally, Lupusregina would have loved to drag this out—to snap a few limbs, watch the proud adamantite hero squirm in the dirt, and savor the transition from confidence to despair. But Lord Ainz’s orders regarding Carne Village echoed sharply in her mind. *No intruders. No leaks. Absolute secrecy.* Worse, she knew from Entoma’s report that this vampire was an arcane caster capable of flight and potentially teleportation. If she toyed with her and let her slip away, she would fail Ainz-sama again.

Evileye reached the exact same conclusion in reverse. She had no intention of fighting a monster of this caliber alone. Her sole objective was survival and reporting this sighting to Lakyus.

Without a word, Evileye snapped her fingers, invoking **[Crystal Wall]** between herself and the maid to block line of sight while simultaneously chanting **[Teleportation]**.

Down below, Lupusregina’s ears twitched at the sudden spike in mana. Her playful demeanor evaporated instantly.

"Oh, no you don't," Lupusregina muttered, her voice dropping an octave into a low, predatory growl.

Before Evileye’s spatial coordinates could even lock into place, Lupusregina kicked off the forest floor. Boosted by her werewolf racial stats and YGGDRASIL-tier divine buffs, the ground beneath her boots exploded into a crater of dirt and shattered roots. She cleared the twenty-foot vertical gap in a fraction of a second, swinging her massive silver crozier in a devastating horizontal arc.

*CRACK.*

Evileye’s **[Crystal Wall]**—a fourth-tier defensive spell designed to withstand siege weaponry—shattered like thin ice on impact. The sheer kinetic force of the blow blasted through the barrier, the wind pressure alone slamming into Evileye and violently disrupting her concentration. The incomplete **[Teleportation]** fizzled out in a shower of harmless sparks.

*Impossible!* Evileye’s mind raced as she banked hard to the left using **[Fly]**, narrowly avoiding the follow-up downward cleave of the giant staff. *She shattered a fourth-tier barrier in a single physical swing! Even Gagaran at full strength couldn't break it that cleanly!*

"That little trick almost worked on Entoma, didn't it?" Lupusregina laughed brightly as she landed effortlessly on a thick tree branch, already coiling her legs to leap again. "Sorry, shorty, I’m not letting you go home!"

Realizing she couldn't outrun the maid's instantaneous bursts of speed without creating distance first, Evileye shifted immediately to lethal force. She raised both hands, pouring her mana into an offensive barrage.

"**[Twin Maximize Magic - Crystal Lance]**!"

Sixteen razor-sharp spears of hardened crystal materialized in the air and screamed toward Lupusregina at blinding velocity. Evileye didn't expect them to kill a monster on par with the Evil Deities, but they had pierced Entoma's armored carapace—they should at least force the maid onto the defensive and buy Evileye the three seconds she needed to cast **[Teleportation]** again.

Lupusregina didn't even bother dodging.

She simply crossed her arms and took the barrage head-on. The maximized crystal lances struck her maid uniform and exposed skin with the sound of shattering glass. A few jagged shards scratched superficially across her cheek and forearms, drawing thin beads of crimson blood that closed up and vanished before they could even drip. Combined with the massive level disparity and the high-tier defensive enchantments woven into her uniform, Evileye’s strongest Earth-elemental attack barely registered as a sting.

Evileye’s blood ran cold. *No damage?! Not even a flinch?!*

In that single instant, Evileye understood the horrifying truth: this woman wasn't merely equal to the bug maid from the capital. She was on an entirely different plane of existence. Fighting her wasn't a battle; it was suicide.

Desperation overriding everything else, Evileye abandoned verbal incantations entirely, activating **[Silent Magic - Teleportation]** while triggering **[Negative Burst]** from her body to buy a split second of breathing room.

A wave of black, necrotic energy erupted outward from Evileye, washing over the surrounding trees and instantly withering the leaves to ash. Lupusregina leaped straight through the dark shockwave, barely slowed by the negative energy, her amber eyes locked onto the faint spatial distortion forming around Evileye’s cloak.

"I said *stay put*!" Lupusregina snarled, raising one hand as divine mana flared around her like a miniature sun. "**[Holy Smite]**!"

A blinding pillar of pure, sacred radiance tore down from the night sky, engulfing Evileye before the silent teleportation could complete.

"GAAAHHH!"

Evileye’s agonized shriek tore through the forest. To a True Vampire, high-tier holy magic wasn't merely painful—it was pure poison. The sacred light seared through her crimson cloak, boiling away her undead flesh, scorching her mask, and completely halting her natural vampire regeneration. Smoke poured from every inch of her body as her spells collapsed entirely, gravity seizing her charred form and dropping her toward the forest floor.

She didn't even reach the ground.

Lupusregina intercepted her mid-fall, swinging her giant crozier with both hands like a battering ram. The heavy **silver** head—a vampire's ultimate bane—connected squarely with Evileye’s chest and shattered mask.

There was no clash of steel, no dramatic final stand. Powered by the overwhelming physical strength of a level-fifty-nine werewolf, the silver weapon crushed Evileye’s ribcage inward, obliterating her undead core in a burst of blue sparks and dissipating mist.

Evileye’s lifeless body slammed into the dirt below, already beginning to dissolve into ash as her vampire existence unraveled.

Lupusregina landed lightly beside the crumbling remains, resting the butt of her silver crozier into the soft earth. She stared down at the fading pile of dust for a moment, a slow, satisfied grin spreading across her face as she pulled a message scroll from her pocket.

"Man, Entoma is gonna be so mad I didn't leave her the vocal cords," Lupusregina hummed cheerfully to the empty woods, before tapping her finger to her ear to report in. "Oh well. Ainz-sama said no witnesses, right?"
